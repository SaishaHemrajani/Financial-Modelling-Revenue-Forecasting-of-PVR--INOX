# PVR INOX – Financial Modelling & Revenue Forecasting

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the financial analysis and revenue forecasting of PVR INOX Pictures Limited, India's multiplex cinema operator.

The study examines historical financial performance through the Profit & Loss Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement, and develops revenue projections for FY2025–26 to FY2029–30 using an assumption-driven Excel financial model.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse historical financial statements to understand the company's financial performance and financial position.
- Examine revenue, operating expenses, profitability and cash flow trends.
- Develop revenue forecasts using operational and business-specific assumptions.
- Evaluate the impact of screen expansion, occupancy rates and pricing trends on projected revenues.
- Understand the key revenue drivers of the multiplex exhibition business.

## Key Areas of Analysis

**1. Historical Financial Statement Analysis**
- Profit & Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Revenue and expense trends
- Operating and financing cash flows

**2. Revenue Forecasting (FY2026–FY2030)**

- Ticket revenue projections based on screen count, seating capacity, occupancy rates, number of shows and average ticket prices.
- Food & Beverage (F&B) revenue forecasting using average spends per head and occupancy assumptions.
- Other operating income projections based on historical revenue contributions.
- Screen expansion assumptions incorporating planned additions over the forecast period.

**3. Financial Modelling & Assumptions**

- Historical growth trends and CAGR calculations.
- Average ticket price growth assumptions.
- F&B spending growth assumptions.
- Screen capacity and occupancy assumptions.
- Driver-based revenue forecasting using Microsoft Excel.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Historical financial analysis, assumption building, formula-driven calculations and revenue forecasting.

## Project Files

- `PVR INOX- solved.xlsx` – Historical financial statements, forecasting assumptions and FY2026–FY2030 revenue projections.

## Data Sources

- PVR INOX historical financial statements.
- Company financial information and historical operating metrics incorporated into the Excel model.

**Historical Financial Data:** FY2018–19 to FY2024–25  
**Projection Period:** FY2025–26 to FY2029–30  
**Reporting Units:** INR Crores, unless otherwise specified.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates an assumption-driven approach to financial modelling by connecting operational business drivers with revenue projections. It highlights how screen expansion, theatre occupancy, ticket pricing and food & beverage spending influence the revenue outlook of a multiplex operator.
